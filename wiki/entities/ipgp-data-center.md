# IPGP Data Center (IPGP-DC)

The data center of the Institut de physique du globe de Paris. It manages and distributes IPGP's geophysical data. Website: http://datacenter.ipgp.fr, contact datacenter@ipgp.fr. Source: [[ipgp-dc-data-access]].

- **History** (slide 4): until the end of 2010 it was the [[geoscope]] Data Center. In 2008 the VOLOBSIS project specified how IPGP's seismological and volcanological data would be integrated. It has been the IPGP Data Center since 2011.
- **Services** (slide 5): long-term archive, distribution through standard interfaces ([[fdsn-web-services]] at `https://ws.ipgp.fr`), web hosting, links to other data centers, DOI declaration (through INIST) and expertise on data formats.
- **Integrated with** (slide 3): Résif, EIDA/ORFEUS, EPOS, [[fdsn]] / IRIS-DMC and NASA's Planetary Data System.
- **Networks distributed** (slides 6–7):
  - G, via the [[geoscope]] portal.
  - The observatory networks PF ([[piton-de-la-fournaise]], run by [[ovpf-ipgp]]), GL, MQ and WI, via the **VOLOBSIS** portal http://volobsis.ipgp.fr. That portal holds GNSS, seismology (validated and real-time), volcanology data and derived products such as volcano reports and events.
  - XB (InSight, Mars).
  - The DOI for each network is listed in [[ipgp-dc-data-access]].
- **Availability pages** (slide 87): http://volobsis.ipgp.fr/inventory.php and http://geoscope.ipgp.fr/index.php/en/data/continuous-data/data-availability.
- **Volcano Reports web service** (slides 89–90): `https://ws.ipgp.fr/volcano-reports/`. It gives access to volcano activity reports and is connected to EPOS Volcano Observations.
