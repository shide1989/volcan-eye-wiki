# GEOSCOPE

A worldwide network of broadband seismic stations (network code G) run from IPGP. [[rer-station]] belongs to it. Data portal: http://geoscope.ipgp.fr. Source: [[jerk-beauducel-2025]] (pp. 6–7, ref. 62). Its channels follow the [[fdsn]] [[seed-channel-naming]] convention.

- **Archive:** its data center became the [[ipgp-data-center]] in 2011 ([[ipgp-dc-data-access]], slide 4). Data is served through [[fdsn-web-services]] at `https://ws.ipgp.fr` (slide 22).
- **DOI:** 10.18715/GEOSCOPE.G (slide 7).
- **Portal contents** (slide 6): seismology, strong motion, temperature and pressure, validated and real-time data, earthquake information, and poles-zeros and response files.
- **Availability page:** http://geoscope.ipgp.fr/index.php/en/data/continuous-data/data-availability (slide 87).
