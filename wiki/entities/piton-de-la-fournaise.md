# Piton de la Fournaise (La Réunion)

A frequently erupting basaltic volcano on La Réunion island, monitored by [[ovpf-ipgp]]. Source: [[jerk-beauducel-2025]].

- **Plumbing:** a shallow magma reservoir about 2 km below the summit. From there, dykes rise vertically toward the summit or travel laterally to the flanks (p. 2).
- **Precursors:** VT, LP and VLP signals are all seen before eruptions. The [[jerk-signal]] also appears as a transient on the horizontal components of [[rer-station]] (p. 2).
- **Eruption counts:** 48 eruptions from 1998 to 2023. Of these, 24 fall in 2014–2023, the real-time Jerk period (pp. 2, 5).
- **Dolomieu summit crater:** sits above a collapsed conduit, where the rock is less coherent. Summit eruptions there (2006, 2008, 2019, 2020) produced weak or late Jerks (pp. 3, 5).
- **Response:** staff are alerted automatically when the number of earthquakes per hour passes a threshold. Authorities are alerted when the swarm becomes intense and comes with rapid deformation. Jerk supports that first-order assessment (p. 5).
- **Seismic network PF:** distributed by the [[ipgp-data-center]] (DOI 10.18715/reunion.PF). The deck's worked example finds stations BON, BOR, ENO, FJS, FLR and FOR (truncated list) within 3 km of the "main crater" at −21.244384, 55.714093 ([[ipgp-dc-data-access]], slides 7, 46, 50). Note that [[rer-station]] is in network G, not PF.
- **Not covered by multi-volcano ML forecasting:** Piton de la Fournaise is not among the 24 volcanoes in [[ardid-dempsey-2025-transfer-learning]], and that study excluded effusive eruptions (p. 8). Its transfer-learning results ([[transfer-learning-eruption-forecasting]]) do not apply here as published.
