# FDSN (International Federation of Digital Seismograph Networks)

A federation of seismic networks. It maintains the SEED format and recommends the channel-naming convention in [[seed-channel-naming]] to its members as a usage standard for exchanging data. It set **N** as the accelerometer instrument code in August 2000. Provenance URLs for derived (`X`) and non-specific (`Y`) channels must point to the FDSN site. Source: [[seed-manual-appendix-a]].

It adopted SEED in 1987. It defines FDSN StationXML, an XML form of the main SEED 2.4 metadata structures with enhancements. It standardizes the [[fdsn-web-services]], whose specification v1.0 dates from 2013 ([[ipgp-dc-data-access]], slides 11, 13, 15, 21).

Website: http://www.fdsn.org/
